- **EV009:** Supplemental paperwork (cash handling/count context).

## Methodology

The following non-destructive procedures were performed as applicable:

1. **Visual survey (unaided and magnified):** Checked for overwriting, erasures, abrasions, ink pooling, and disturbed paper fibers.
2. **Side-light inspection:** Looked for impressions, indentation differences, and surface disruption consistent with erasure or scraping.
3. **Ink/line assessment (comparative only):** Compared relative ink tone and line quality across entries on the same page to identify potentially different writing instruments or writing times.
4. **Numeral and handwriting feature comparison:** Compared repeated numerals, letter forms, spacing, baseline habits, and pen pressure patterns within and across pages when similar entry types were present.
5. **Sequence and completeness review:** Checked for missing pages, inconsistent pagination, and mismatched form fields (e.g., totals not aligning with subtotals).

No chemical ink dating, ESDA processing, or destructive fiber testing was performed.

## Results

- Across **EV001**, several fields contained **numerical entries with differing line quality** (e.g., one set appearing darker/heavier and another lighter/finer) within the same document set. These differences are **consistent with use of different writing instruments and/or separate writing events**, but are not, by themselves, proof of alteration.
- In **EV004** and **EV005**, at least one total or subtotal field showed **localized overwriting/correction behavior** (e.g., re-traced numerals and tightened loops) compared to surrounding entries. Under magnification, the corrected areas showed **increased ink density** relative to adjacent writing.
- On select pages within **EV001** and **EV009**, there were **minor surface disturbances** near corrected numerals (consistent with light erasure or rubbing). The disturbances were limited in size and not present uniformly across the page.
- The layout and printing of the forms within **EV001** appeared **consistent within the packet** (similar font and alignment), with no obvious indications of page substitution detected during this examination.
- No clear evidence of cut-and-paste assembly, page splicing, or mechanically removed sections was observed on the submitted originals.

All observations above are limited to the submitted items and their condition at the time of examination.

## Interpretation

The observed differences in ink appearance and the presence of corrections in **EV001**, **EV004**, and **EV005** indicate that some entries were likely completed at different times and/or with different writing instruments. Minor surface disturbance near certain corrected numerals supports the possibility of light erasure or rubbing prior to rewriting.

These findings support that the paperwork includes **edited or corrected entries** rather than being uniformly “single-pass” writing. However, this examination **does not determine** who made any entry, when (beyond relative sequencing), or whether any correction was authorized. No opinion is offered regarding intent or responsibility.
