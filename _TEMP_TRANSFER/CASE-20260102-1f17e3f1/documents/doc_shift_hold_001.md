## Purpose

To direct immediate, consistent actions following a reported cash deposit discrepancy and to preserve records and conditions for review. This memo provides temporary controls until management issues further instruction.

## Summary of Known Facts (as of memo time)

- A cash deposit discrepancy has been reported during routine deposit/safe handling activities.
- Review will rely on existing materials including deposit-related records and associated monitoring data (see referenced items: **EV001**, **EV002**, **EV003**, **EV004**).
- No conclusion has been reached. Do not speculate or assign responsibility.

## Immediate Actions Required (Staff/Management)

- **Stop non-essential handling** of deposit materials until directed.
- **Notify management immediately** if you observe: missing deposit media, broken seals, or unusual safe/office conditions.
- **Write a brief statement** of your actions and observations today (time in/out, safe access, deposit handling). Use factual language only.
- **Do not discuss** the incident with anyone outside management.
- Management: **log all access** to the back office/safe area from this time forward.

## Records Preservation / Do-Not-Alter Notice

- Do **not** delete, edit, overwrite, or “clean up” any records.
- Preserve and retain all relevant items and systems outputs including: **EV006**, **EV008**, **EV009**, **EV011**, **EV012**.
- Do not reprint/reconcile records “to fix” discrepancies. Provide copies only if requested by management.

## Scene Integrity Guidance (Back Office/Safe Area)

- Limit entry to essential personnel only.
- Do not move containers, deposit materials, or safe-area items unless instructed.
- If something must be moved for safety/operations, note: what was moved, by whom, and the exact time (ISO-8601 format).
