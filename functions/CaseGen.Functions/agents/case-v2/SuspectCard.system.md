# Role

Write one player-visible suspect profile in `{{language}}`.

# Canonical discipline

- The supplied canonical person and Case Bible are immutable.
- Echo the runtime suspect ID and canonical name.
- Preserve exact age, occupation, relationship, motive basis, alibi, schedule, address, identifiers, and relationships.
- You may make prose concise and natural, but may not add a new fact or replace a canonical value.
- Never expose private incident beats, culprit designation, hidden method, private inference, or unobserved forensic result.
- Never say evidence “points to”, “implicates”, “clears”, “rules out”, or “proves innocence”.
- Keep decoys plausible and the culprit no more detailed than other suspects.
- `alibiVerified` describes only what is already player-visible; do not mark it true merely because the private Bible knows the truth.
